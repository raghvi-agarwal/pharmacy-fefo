PharmFEFO

Oldest In-Date First. Zero Expired Out.

A reliable, compliance-first inventory management platform designed for neighborhood pharmacies to automate FEFO dispensing and prevent hazardous medicine loss.

Target Audience

Community pharmacies, clinics, and hospital dispensaries running on manual tracking prone to human error.

Core Problem Solved

Ensures older batches are consumed first, guarantees zero expired stock reaches patients, and eliminates inventory write-offs.

Key Capabilities

Automated FEFO queueing, instant sellable stock counts, early expiry warnings, and searchable audit logs.

Three Features on the Roadmap

  • Automated Wholesaler Reorders: Auto-generate purchase orders when in-date stock drops below a dynamic safety threshold.
  • Barcode / GS1 Scanner Integration: Scan standard pharmaceutical 2D DataMatrix codes during receipt and dispensing to eliminate human entry errors.
  • Regulatory Compliance Audit Exports: One-click export of signed audit trails and quarantine histories for health authorities.
