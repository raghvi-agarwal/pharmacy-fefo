PharmFEFO

Logged in as:
Autonomous Inventory

Oldest In-Date First. Zero Expired Out.

A compliance-first pharmacy operating system that automates First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO) dispensing, guarantees no expired batch reaches patients, and alerts staff ahead of shelf-life expiry.

🎯 Target Audience

Neighborhood pharmacies, hospital dispensary counters, and clinics managing multi-batch inventory.

🛡️ Problem Solved

Eliminates dangerous accidental dispensing of expired stock and minimizes shelf write-off financial losses.

⚡ Key Features

Automatic FEFO multi-batch splitting, real-time in-date stock ledger, 30-day early warnings, and audit logging.

Future Roadmap:
  1. Automated Wholesaler Reordering: Auto-dispatch purchase orders when valid stock hits safety buffer.
  2. 2D GS1 Barcode Matrix Scanning: Direct hardware scanner support for single-second batch intake.
  3. Regulatory Audit Exports: Export encrypted PDF compliance reports for pharmaceutical regulatory reviews.
